How these numbers are made

Prints are recorded trades. Buy and sell are inferred aggressor sides (quote midpoint within one second, else tick rule); unknown volume is kept separate. Dealer flow assumes dealers took the passive side of every signed print (S−B); 50% participation is an illustrative fixed assumption, not an estimate. Endpoints assign all unknowns to buyers or to sellers, and are scenario bounds, not confidence intervals; direction is marked uncertain when they include zero. Opening dealer inventory is unknown: the conditional total range adds −OI…+OI of the latest settled open interest for each exact contract, assumes no net change between settlement and the open, and is applied once. Values use the gamma and spot of one chain snapshot at or before the displayed time; a snapshot older than two minutes or a missing field is shown as no coverage, never as zero. Only the captured contracts are covered.
