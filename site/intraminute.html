Pressure is (call buys − call sells − put buys + put sells) divided by quote-signed contracts. Each selected interval is classified from its own first-to-last observed midpoint move, in spread units, and its signed flow; the midpoint change one second later is a separate next-interval observation and never changes that interval's classification. All panels share collector receipt time. The amber curve is the last observed QQQ midpoint in each interval, with bid and ask in blue. Trade ranges use uncorrected regular-sale (@) prints as received. Option direction uses the existing q1 and q5 quote rules. Other signing bases remain visible in the tape. Gray bars count unclassified contracts. Hatched intervals mark recorded outages. The premium lane plots, per interval, the size-weighted change in option print price (dollars per share) from each contract's first print received this minute, calls in magenta and puts in sky blue; faint whiskers span that interval's lowest to highest change, and points join across empty intervals but never across an outage. Every priced print counts, signed or not. With All strikes the lane mixes contracts, so choose a strike to follow one call and one put. Premium is displayed beside the flow and is not an input to the Alkalus label.

How to read the preliminary flow labels

Descriptive only, not a trading signal or an option-payoff measure. Golden time means at least 5 spreads of midpoint movement with signed flow; working copper means 1 to under 5. Lead means less than 1 spread, balanced flow, or a move against the flow; the reason is shown beside the label. Missing midpoint, positive spread, signed flow, or continuous coverage leaves the interval unclassified. Spread means the last observed ask minus bid within this interval.
