Bayesian minute-end regime

Estimate where the QQQ midpoint will finish relative to the previous candle’s plotted midpoint range. Turning on Underlying trades includes the plotted trade range. Choose Manual to enter your own bounds. The known time applies to the volatility and any manual bounds.

Model probabilities assume normally distributed price increments. One-minute volatility is your assumed standard deviation of price noise. The unknown one-minute drift starts at zero in expectation with that same prior standard deviation. Available quotes update the drift. These probabilities describe the closing midpoint relative to the band; calibration and directional persistence require separate testing.

How to read the preliminary flow labels

Descriptive only, not a trading signal or an option-payoff measure. Golden time means at least 5 spreads of midpoint movement with signed flow; working copper means 1 to under 5. Lead means less than 1 spread, balanced flow, or a move against the flow; the reason is shown beside the label. Missing midpoint, positive spread, signed flow, or continuous coverage leaves the interval unclassified. Spread means the last observed ask minus bid within this interval.
