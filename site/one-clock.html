Strategy Archive hypothesis

Compare each pair of consecutive one-minute candles. A green candle must exceed the preceding red high, or a red candle must break the preceding green low. Measure the signed change to the close exactly five minutes later. Replay the October 29 reference session.

Strike encounters

The session opening map sets each strike’s group using settled open interest and the opening ATM. Encounters qualify once the opening map was available. A fresh approach after leaving the strike can count again.

The first eligible endpoint beyond the strike in the approach direction counts as continuation. An endpoint on the approach side counts as rejection. A price exactly at the strike counts as a tie.
