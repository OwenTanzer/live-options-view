One clock (New York) for every lane; each underlying and each option has its own price scale. Archived quotes are separate observations from the journal's fills: lines and bands are continuous archived coverage around the fills; dots and ticks are sparse samples of the holding period; blank stretches were not read.

Fills

Times are the journal's execution-alert or broker order-initiation times, not exchange fill times. Fees are estimated until actual charges are recorded.
